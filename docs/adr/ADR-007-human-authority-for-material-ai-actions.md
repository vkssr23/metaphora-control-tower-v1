# ADR-007: Human authority for material AI actions

Status: Accepted for future systems; no AI implemented

Context: Future AI may assist freight operations but can be wrong or manipulated.

Decision: AI may recommend or prepare. Material safety, financial, external, or irreversible actions require controlled authority/approval under a future autonomy policy. Record agent/version, model where relevant, input provenance, tool calls, approval, cost/usage, and evaluated outcome.

Consequences: Autonomy advances gradually; no Level-5 claims.

Alternatives considered: Unbounded autonomous authority was rejected.

Future review trigger: A separately approved Agent Tool & Approval Plane phase begins.
